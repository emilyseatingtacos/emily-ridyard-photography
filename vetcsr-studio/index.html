Product architecture

One knowledge system, many useful outputs.

AI accelerated clustering, rewriting, consistency checks, and information architecture. My hospital experience determined what was realistic, what was safe, and what a busy CSR could actually use.

How I built it

Experience supplied the judgment. AI supplied leverage.

  1. 01
    Extract recurring moments

    Identify the calls, handoffs, questions, and emotional pressure points that repeatedly challenge veterinary front desks.

  2. 02
    Structure the knowledge

    Use AI to cluster notes, compare phrasings, surface gaps, and turn tacit experience into reusable content fields.

  3. 03
    Apply human safety review

    Remove diagnostic language, promises, invented certainty, and anything that conflicts with clinic-specific protocols.

  4. 04
    Design for the moment of use

    Compress the result into pages, prompts, and scripts that remain scannable when phones, clients, and clinical teams all need attention.
